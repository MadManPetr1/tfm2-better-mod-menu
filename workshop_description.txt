[h1]Better Mod Menu[/h1]

[b]A clearer, faster mod manager for Teamfight Manager 2.[/b]

Search, inspect, configure, and troubleshoot installed mods through a dedicated interface while preserving the game's native mod system.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*] Search installed mods by name or author.
[*] Filter All, Local, and Workshop mods.
[*] Scroll long mod lists with the mouse wheel or keyboard.
[*] Enable or disable individual mods or apply a bulk action.
[*] Review dependency, version, update, and restart warnings.
[*] View thumbnails, banners, summaries, settings, and author profiles.
[*] Use optional manifest-driven settings and actions provided by compatible mods.
[/list]

Mods do not need Better Mod Menu integration to appear or remain manageable.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[b]Tested with:[/b] TFM2 0.5.3

Compatibility with later versions is not guaranteed until confirmed.

[h2]Setup[/h2]

[list]
[*] Subscribe to and enable Better Mod Menu.
[*] Restart the game when prompted.
[*] Open Mods from the title screen.
[/list]

Enabling, disabling, or updating native mods may still require a restart.

[h2]For mod authors[/h2]

Optional integration uses at most two files:

[code]better_mod_menu.json
better_mod_menu_profile.json[/code]

Schemas, examples, and the modder guide are available in the GitHub repository.
